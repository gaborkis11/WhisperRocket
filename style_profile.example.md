# Style profile (example / template)

Copy this to `~/.config/whisperrocket/style_profile.md` and fill it in, or just
click **Edit** in Settings → AI → Style profile and WhisperRocket seeds the same
template for you.

The profile tells the AI cleanup how *you* write, so it keeps your voice instead
of flattening it into generic corporate prose. It is read on every dictation and
never modified by the app.

**Keep it aggregate.** Describe traits, not examples. Do not paste real messages,
names, addresses or anything else you would not want sent to a model. This file
is deliberately gitignored and is never committed.

Delete the questions and leave your answers.

## Sentence length and rhythm

(Short and clipped, or long and flowing? Do you use dashes, ellipses, one-line
paragraphs?)

## Greetings and sign-offs

(Do you open with a greeting, or start straight into the message? Do you sign
off, and how?)

## Language mixing

(Do you mix in English or other loanwords? Which kinds — technical terms, slang,
filler?)

## Swearing

(Where and how do you swear? Which words? These are kept **verbatim** — the
cleanup never softens, censors or substitutes them, and the output guard rejects
any response that tries.)

## Formality

(How do you address people? Formal, informal, does it depend on who?)

## Anything else

(Habits worth knowing: how you write numbers and dates, whether you use emoji,
capitalisation quirks.)
